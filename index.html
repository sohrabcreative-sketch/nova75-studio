Your NOVA75, configured in the browser.

Lighting, per-key colours, key remapping, the screen and keyboard settings — sent straight from this page to the keyboard over WebHID. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your computer.

This keyboard does not identify itself as a NOVA75. Other keyboards share the same USB identity but have different key positions and screen limits. Writing is locked until you confirm.

Connected keyboard

Available on this connection

    USB cable

    Everything: lighting effects, per-key colours, both key layers, image and GIF upload to the 240×135 screen, clock sync and settings. Put the switch on the back in the wired position.

    2.4G receiver

    Battery level, lighting effect, sleep timer, key response and game mode. Keys, per-key colours and the screen need the cable — the official driver has the same limit.

    Before you write

    The keyboard can't report most of its configuration, so this page shows what it last sent from this browser. The protocol comes from the official driver as documented by the OpenKey project; treat writes as experimental until you've seen each one work on your board.

    Lighting

    Choose an effect and press Apply. Each Apply saves to the keyboard's memory.

    Per-key colours

    Pick a colour, then click or drag across keys. Writing switches the keyboard to its custom-colour mode.

    Per-key colours need the USB cable.

    Keys

    Click a key to change what it does. Changes stay on this page until you write the layer to the keyboard. The Fn key itself can't be changed.

    Before the first write
    
      
    
        This is an unofficial tool. Its commands are the ones the official driver sends, but they have not been
        verified on NOVA75 hardware by this app's authors. It never sends firmware-update, reset or erase commands.
      
    
      
    If something looks wrong afterwards, the official AULA driver can restore the keyboard's settings.
